Cookie policy

Does this site use cookies?

No. glpmurcia.com sets no first-party or third-party cookies for advertising or tracking purposes, which is why no consent banner is shown.

Cookieless statistics

Visits are measured with GoatCounter, which works without cookies and without identifying users.

Installable app

If you install the website as an app on your phone, the browser keeps a local copy of some pages so they work offline. It contains no personal data and can be cleared from your browser settings.

Social media links

Links to Facebook, X or Telegram are plain links: no content from those platforms is loaded until you click them. From then on, their own cookie policies apply.
