Masonic Orders

Under the auspices of the Provincial Grand Lodge of Murcia, various Masonic Orders work, each with their own rites, traditions and degrees. Below are the bodies currently active in our Province.

Masonic Order

Mark Master Masons

One of the most widespread additional degrees in the Masonic tradition. Its symbolism revolves around the keystone of Solomon's Temple: it illustrates how judgement based on appearance can reject the most important piece of the building. It regards the brethren as "living stones" in the building of an inner, spiritual temple, and imparts the lesson of just reward for effort and merit.

◆

The historic journey towards Masonic perfection.

Masonic Order

Royal Ark Mariners

An ancient and honourable Fraternity whose ceremony is set around the Universal Flood. Its teachings focus on the virtues of obedience and faith practised by the Patriarch Noah in response to the divine command to build the Ark to preserve humanity. It has worked under the jurisdiction of the Grand Lodge of Mark Master Masons since 1871.

◆

Obedience and faith in the face of the divine command for the salvation of humanity.

Masonic Order

Order of Athelstan

Founded in honour of King Athelstan of England, the first Christian monarch who granted a founding charter to Freemasonry in 926 AD at York. Its motto — Honor virtutis praemium — proclaims that honour is the reward of virtue, the guiding principle of all its workings.

◆

Honour as the supreme reward of virtue and work well done.

Masonic Order

Holy Royal Arch of Jerusalem

Considered the essential completion of the degree of Master Mason, it is the Supreme Order of Freemasonry. Its roots trace back to 1743 in Ireland and in 1813, with the Grand Union, it was officially incorporated as an indispensable part of "pure and ancient" Freemasonry. Its workings are carried out in Chapters.

◆

The completion of Freemasonry: the rediscovery of the Lost Word.

Masonic Order

United Religious and Military Orders

Full name: The United Religious, Military and Masonic Orders of the Temple, of St John of Jerusalem, Palestine, Rhodes and Malta. Its historical roots date back to 1118 AD, when the Order was founded in Jerusalem to protect pilgrims in the Holy Land. In its modern Masonic form, the candidate assumes the role of a pilgrim who is elevated to the dignity of Knight. Its workings are carried out in Preceptories.

◆

Courage, inner reflection and action: the elements of life's pilgrimage.

Masonic Order

Order of the Secret Monitor

Full name: Brotherhoods of David and Jonathan in the British Isles and Overseas Territories. Based on the bond of friendship and loyalty that united King David and Jonathan according to the Scriptures, this Order celebrates the values of unconditional fidelity and sincere brotherhood. Its workings take place in Councils, assembled in the Grand Conclave.

◆

Loyalty and true friendship, a reflection of the eternal bond between David and Jonathan.

Masonic Order

Allied Masonic Degrees

The Order of Allied Masonic Degrees brings together several degrees of different traditions and origins which, though not belonging to a single system, share the spirit of fraternal union. Its motto — Hominum Proposita Deus Constituit — proclaims that it is God who orders the purposes of man. Its workings take place in Councils, presided over by the Grand Council.

◆

Hominum Proposita Deus Constituit — It is God who orders the purposes of man.

Masonic Order

Knights Templar Priests

The Grand College of the Holy Royal Arch Knight Templar Priests and Order of Holy Wisdom finds its roots in the 18th-century Irish Masonic tradition. Its mysteries unfold in the Celestial Tabernacle and the Priesthood of Melchizedek, exploring the deepest and most spiritual dimension of the Templar tradition. Its bodies of working are known as Tabernacles.

◆

A unique Masonic tradition, consecrated to the mysteries of the Priesthood and Wisdom.

Masonic Order

Supreme Council of the 33rd Degree

The Supreme Council of the 33rd and Last Degree of the Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite for Spain is the supreme authority of the AASR in Spanish territory. Guardian of the philosophical degrees from the 4th to the 33rd, it watches over the regularity and purity of the Rite and is the sole competent body to confer the 33rd Degree, the highest distinction of the Scottish Rite. Its motto — Deus Meumque Jus — proclaims God and Justice as guiding principles.

◆

Deus Meumque Jus — God and My Right, guiding principles of the Scottish Rite.

Collateral Orders Corner

Recent Chronicles

Recent activity of the collateral Lodges and Chapters working under the auspices of the Provincial Grand Lodge of Murcia.

Council Thader No. 240 — Allied Masonic Degrees

22 October 2026

The October Installation meeting of Council Thader No. 240 brought its members together at the Orient of Murcia for a programme of particular significance. The proceedings included two Admission Ceremonies — to the degree of Knight of Constantinople and to the Order of the Weavers of Solomon — followed by the formal Installation of the incoming Venerable Master for 2026-2027, together with the Treasurer and the Inner Guard. The Treasurer's Report, Propositions and general Council business completed the evening.

Tabernacle Virgen de la Arrixaca No. 293 — KTP

20 October 2026

Tabernacle Virgen de la Arrixaca No. 293 of the Grand College of the Holy Royal Arch Knight Templar Priests met at Camposol Club de Golf, Mazarrón, under a temporary venue dispensation. The meeting included the Investiture of officers who were absent at Installation, a practice of the Knight Templar Priest Ceremony in preparation for a new candidate expected in April, and discussion of a change in the regular meeting day to avoid clashes with Craft and Allied bodies. Propositions and the Charity collection also formed part of the proceedings.

Chapter Arroyo de Gihón No. 38 — Royal Arch

16 October 2026

Two milestones defined the meeting of Chapter Arroyo de Gihón No. 38 at the Orient of Murcia: the Exaltation to the degree and the Investiture of the new officers for 2026-2027. The working, convened by the Grand Provincial Chapter of Valencia, also included the presentation of Certificates, Propositions for the Royal Arch, and the circulation of the Alms Box.

GER ANAT Conclave 567 — Secret Monitor

18 September 2026

Two new brethren were Inducted into the Order of the Secret Monitor during the meeting of GER ANAT Conclave 567 at the Orient of Murcia. The proceedings also included elections for 2026-2027 — returning the new Supreme Ruler, Treasurer, and Sentinel — and concluded with the Installation and Commission Ceremony of the new Supreme Ruler, presided over by R∴W∴Bro∴ Luis Alcaina Guzmán, and the installation of the officer team for the coming year.

Siete Coronas Royal Ark Mariners No. 1957

19 June 2026

Officer installations and instruction on the Tracing Board of the Degree: with that twofold agenda, the Royal Ark Mariners Lodge Siete Coronas No. 1957 held its ordinary meeting at the Orient of Murcia. The instructional session, aimed at deepening the symbolism of the Degree, was conducted according to the Order's own ritual.

Siete Coronas Mark Master Masons No. 1957

19 June 2026

Worshipful Master, Treasurer, and Inner Guard: those were the offices filled at the Election Meeting of the Mark Master Masons Lodge Siete Coronas No. 1957, held at the Orient of Murcia. The meeting closed on a particularly warm note, with the presentation of Diplomas to six brethren recently admitted into the Order.
