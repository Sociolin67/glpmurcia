Lodge Life

Chronicles of the meetings and activities of the lodges working under the auspices of the Provincial Grand Lodge of Murcia.

2026–2027 Year
11 September 2026 Moriá No. 143

R∴L∴S∴ Moriá No. 143 opens the 2026-2027 year with its Installation Meeting

The Respectable Symbolic Lodge Moriá No. 143 held its Installation Meeting at the Orient of Murcia, opening the 2026-2027 year under the Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite. During the works, the new Worshipful Master was invested and the officers for the coming year were installed. The meeting concluded with a fraternal agape attended by a significant number of visiting brethren from other Lodges and rites, reflecting the bonds of fraternity that unite the workshops of the Province.

4 September 2026 Fraternidad Mediterránea No. 94

R∴L∴S∴ Fraternidad Mediterránea No. 94 opens the 2026-2027 year with its Installation Meeting

The Respectable Symbolic Lodge Fraternidad Mediterránea No. 94 opened the 2026-2027 year at the Orient of Cartagena with its Installation Meeting, held under the Emulation Rite. The central act was the investiture of the new Worshipful Master and the installation of the officers for the coming year, marking the handover of office with which the Lodge resumes its work after the summer recess.

2025–2026 Year
26 June 2026 Conde de Floridablanca No. 164

R∴L∴S∴ Conde de Floridablanca No. 164 holds its last meeting before the summer recess

The Respectable Symbolic Lodge Conde de Floridablanca No. 164 held its last meeting of the 2025-2026 year at the Orient of Murcia, before the summer recess, under the Emulation Rite. During the works, officers were elected for the 2026-2027 year: Worshipful Master, Treasurer, Inner Guard, and Conciliator. The highlight of the meeting was the Initiation Ceremony of a candidate, who thereby joined the Order. The work concluded with the circulation of the Widow's Box.
